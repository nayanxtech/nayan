Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Nothing comes back to usNo account, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry. Nayan never reports what you do.
Your browsing stays on this PCHistory, bookmarks, cookies and cache live in your own Windows profile.
Passwords are locked by WindowsSaved passwords are encrypted by Windows itself and tied to your Windows account. They are never uploaded.
Ads and trackers blockedA blocking list is refreshed about once a week. Nothing about you is sent when it is.

What leaves your computer, and why

Only what browsing needs: the web pages you choose to open, and these:

Searching. What you type in the address bar goes to the search engine you have chosen — Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or Ecosia — along with the language and country you picked, so that results come back in your language.

Suggestions. While you type, the same search engine is asked for suggestions. You can switch this off in Settings › Search, and it never runs in a private window.

Your AI assistant. If you choose one, its website is opened in the side panel and what you ask is typed into it. That company then holds the conversation under its own policy.

Blocking rules. The ad and tracker list is downloaded from Ghostery roughly weekly. The request carries nothing about you.

Updates. Nayan asks GitHub a few times a day whether a newer version exists. That request carries nothing but the version you already have.

What Nayan keeps on your computer

History, bookmarks, downloads, cookies, cache, your settings, page thumbnails, and any passwords you ask it to remember. All of it sits in your Windows user profile and can be cleared from Settings › Privacy, or removed completely by uninstalling Nayan.

Private windows

A private window keeps no history, no cookies, no thumbnails and no passwords. When the last private window closes, everything it held is thrown away.

Camera, microphone and location

A website gets none of these until you say yes, and your answer is remembered for that one site only. You can review or withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy.

Safer connections

Nayan tries the secure (https) version of every address first, and warns you before falling back.

Children

Nayan is not aimed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new wording arrives with the next version of Nayan and appears on this page.
